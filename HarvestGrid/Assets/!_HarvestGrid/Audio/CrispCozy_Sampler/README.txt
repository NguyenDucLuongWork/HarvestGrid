CRISP COZY - Free Sampler
=========================

20 soft, cute sound effects for cozy game menus, rewards, pickups and everyday actions.

GET THE FULL PACK
  This free sampler has 20 of the 261 sounds.
  The full pack adds the other 241: https://anipixels.itch.io/crisp-cozy

FORMATS
  WAV/  44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (for editing / highest quality)
  OGG/  Ogg Vorbis (small files, ready for Godot, Unity, GameMaker, web)

Every sound is loudness-matched, starts on the first sample (no dead air)
and peaks at or below -1 dBFS.

TIPS
  - Sounds ending in _01, _02, _03 are variations: pick one at random each time
  -     so repeated taps, footsteps and pickups don't sound robotic.
  - A tiny random pitch change (0.96x - 1.04x) makes talk_* and coin spam feel alive.
  - hover_*, scroll_tick, knob_turn, slider_tick and loading_tick are mixed quiet on purpose; they play constantly.
  - Everything is tuned to C major pentatonic (C D E G A), so any two sounds played together stay in tune -
  -     and the pack mixes with Crisp UI, Crisp Puzzle and Crisp Jingles.
  - star_01..03, combo_up_01..05, slider_tick_01..05 and countdown_3, _2, _1, countdown_go climb in order.
  - Pairs are tuned to match: toggle_on/off, switch_on/off, checkbox_on/off, menu_open/close, popup_open/close,
  -     panel_slide_in/out, drawer_open/close, bag_open/close, book_open/close, door_open/close, undo/redo,
  -     cart_add/remove, equip/unequip.
  - talk_low / talk_mid / talk_high / talk_squeak are 'animal talk' syllables: play one per letter or two.
